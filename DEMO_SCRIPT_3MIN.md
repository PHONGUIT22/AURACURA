## 💡 Production Tips for the Recording

1. **Audio**: Use a dedicated USB condenser microphone with noise suppression enabled.
2. **Display**: Set display resolution to 1920x1080 (100% scaling) for razor-sharp typography.
3. **Cursor Pacing**: Move smoothly between cards without rapid jerking; pause for 1 second on each metric before speaking.
4. **Interactive Highlight**: Ensure the Before/After slider is dragged at least twice to show the smooth fluid interaction.
5. **PDF Verification**: Have the exported PDF open in a background tab so clicking preview is instant and seamless.
